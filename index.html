Credit math by company

USD. Run-rate EBITDA = 4 × latest-quarter adjusted EBITDA (Oracle: TTM). Lease view: debt + on-balance-sheet lease liabilities, EBITDAR (adds 4 × quarterly operating-lease cost) over interest + rent; uncommenced leases are listed in the drawer, never added. 24M cover = liquidity ÷ principal due within ~24 months.
