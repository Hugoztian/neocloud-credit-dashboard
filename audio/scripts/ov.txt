This is the Overview of the Neocloud Credit Dashboard, as of 22 November 2026, Singapore time. It covers 18 companies: 13 public or I-P-O-pending names verified against their latest S-E-C filings, and 5 privates sourced from dated news or management releases.

The names we track for debt carry about 118 billion dollars of principal. Six of the 11 scored companies have under 6 months of runway on unrestricted cash plus committed lines: CoreWeave, Nebius, Applied Digital, Cipher, TeraWulf, and Core Scientific. Only CoreWeave has 24-month maturity cover below 1 times — about 0.73 times on the 13.4 billion dollars due by the end of 2027. The median A-I bond spread, as of 19 November, is 437 basis points, versus 248 for the high-yield J-N-K E-T-F. CoreWeave's median spread is about 1,051 basis points. Off-balance-sheet A-I obligations at 7 firms exceed 3 trillion dollars, per the Federal Reserve.

Our conclusions: funding, not demand, is the constraint. CoreWeave has about 4 months of runway and Nebius about 3. Ring-fenced cash is not corporate cash — Hut 8 has about 11 months corporately, versus about 68 months if you count its 6.39 billion dollars of restricted cash and investments. The market prices risk more harshly than the agencies: Oracle's median spread is 318 basis points despite a triple-B-band rating. Nebius's headline score of 61 is flattered by 325 million dollars of one-off revenue; on recurring EBITDA, leverage rises to about 16 times and the score falls to 27. Former miners such as Cipher, TeraWulf and White Fiber still have about zero or negative EBITDA, so their credit rests on tenant backstops. Headline scores range from SpaceX at 86 and Oracle at 85, down to White Fiber at 13 and Applied Digital at 16. This is not investment advice.
