The differences matter more for systemic risk. Who holds the debt scores only 25: the paper sits mostly with non-bank, long-dated investors, and large-bank A-I loans outstanding are about 150 billion dollars, not 40-to-1 banks funded overnight. The ultimate payers are hyperscalers and well-funded labs, not stretched households, although most U-S hyperscalers turned free-cash-flow negative in 2026, according to the European Central Bank.

On scale: A-I-company debt is about 3.6 percent of U-S G-D-P, or up to about 7.3 percent including lease commitments. That is comparable to subprime at 8.3 percent of G-D-P in 2007, but only a fraction of all home mortgages, which were 76.5 percent of G-D-P.
