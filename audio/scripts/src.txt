This is the Sources and method tab. Every public-company figure is checked against the latest S-E-C filing — a 10-Q, 10-K, 6-K, 8-K or S-1. The filing wins over news. Each figure is labelled Reported, Management, News, or Calculation. Private companies have no filings, so those figures are dated news or company releases, at lower confidence, or marked not available.

All credit math lives in one engine, used by this page and by the data build. Headline net debt subtracts only unrestricted cash. Liquidity is unrestricted cash plus committed undrawn facilities usable at quarter-end. Restricted cash is added only on the project-funded basis. Headline figures use strict quarter-end balances; the pro-forma toggle adds post-quarter raises symmetrically, such as Oracle's 10 billion dollars of notes and 10 billion of equity in November.
